# Weather data

This directory contains the monthly station extracts used by the notebook:

- `ALL-WEATHER-STATIONS.csv` — station names, locations, record dates, and file identifiers.
- 37 station CSV files — monthly maximum/minimum temperature, air-frost days, rainfall, sunshine, and record status.

Source: UK Met Office, [Historic station data](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/maps-and-data/historic-station-data).

The records were downloaded in 2025 and may differ from the continuously updated source. Missing observations are represented as `N/A` and are retained as missing during analysis. Users should review the [Met Office legal and licensing information](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/policies/legal) before reuse.
